### Independent architecture scoring

Two fresh Sol seats that did not author the options scored a fixed five-criterion rubric: immediate Air value 30%, footprint 20%, authority/privacy 25%, seamless use/availability 15%, effort/reversibility 10%. Anchors are preserved in `architecture-options.md`. Scores express judgement of the proposals; they do not validate estimates or performance. Both seats are the same model/family, so agreement is weaker evidence than independent cross-family review. No additional provider was used.

Both prefer H over E and F: existing indexes, small client footprint and retained writers outweigh the immediate host dependency. Their strongest objection is identical: Pro availability is temporary and Mini serving readiness is unproven. E/F differences are far smaller than score uncertainty; neither their mean ranking nor the decimal precision should determine that choice. Per-criterion reasons, pairwise comparisons and objections are retained verbatim in `judge-one.md` and `judge-two.md`. No iterative climb or owner verdict occurred; costs/tokens per seat were not exposed and remain unknown.
